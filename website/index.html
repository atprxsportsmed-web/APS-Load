Why 18

Most golfers piece improvement together from a range, a gym, a coach and a rehab clinic that never talk to each other. We put the whole thing in one building.

The 18 Method

Know your body. Know your swing. Measure the change.

Built by an expert in health and human performance, so you have the tools to play your best for as long as possible.

Week 0 · Test

Your 18 Profile

An interview and a full golf-specific assessment of how you move, how strong you are and how you swing. It shows where your body and your swing limit each other.

Weeks 1–12 · Train

Your custom routine

A routine built from your profile, run on the hitting stations and the training floor whenever it suits you, with your pros working from the same plan.

Week 12 · Retest

See your progress

We retest every 3 months, update your profile and adjust the plan. Progress you can see, not guess at.

    Questions

    Before you inquire

    When does the club open?

    We'll announce the opening date once it's set. Founding members hear first about the location, tours and the opening date.

    What does "founding member" mean?

    The first 25 members to commit before opening. Your spot is held when the club opens, and you're first in for your 18 Profile assessment. A founding commitment is non-binding until we open.

    How does 24/7 access work?

    Members get keycard entry at any hour. The building has cameras, and every member signs a waiver.

    Can juniors train here?

    Yes, through junior session packages. Minors train only with a parent or coach present.

    Is the 18 Profile only for low handicaps?

    No. The testing shows where your body helps or limits your swing at any level, and your routine starts from where you are.

    Why is there a membership cap?

    At 100 members you can always get on a station and your pros know your plan. The cap keeps it that way.
