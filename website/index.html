Digital strength that adapts to every rep

Digital strength: every rep tracked, every session adapting to you

Strength training is where most golfers' plans fall apart. A generic routine can't tell whether you're fresh, tired, stronger than last month, or favoring one side. Our digital strength stations can.

Each station is a Tonal 2 smart strength system. Your 18 Profile becomes a routine built for your swing and your goals. Then the station measures every rep and responds to it. Resistance adjusts as you perform, so the load fits how you're moving at that moment, not how you moved last month.

  • Built from your 18 Profile. Exact routines for your goals, not a template.
  • Every rep tracked. Load, speed and power are recorded, so progress is measured, not guessed.
  • Adapts in real time. Resistance responds to your performance during the set.
  • Retested every 3 months. Your numbers show what changed, and the routine updates with them.
  • Available 24/7. High-level, individualized strength work on your schedule, any time of day, any day of the week.

One place. One plan. A plan that adjusts to you on every rep.

Questions

Before you join the list

When does the club open?

Our target is fall 2027 (October or November) in downtown Chattanooga. It's a target, not a promise. People on the waitlist hear first when the date is set.

What does joining the waitlist mean?

You'll hear first about the opening, tours and membership. It's free, and no payment or deposit is taken.

Why a 3-month commitment?

That's one full cycle: interview and testing, a custom routine, then a retest at the end of the quarter so you can see the change.

Can I save with a longer term?

Yes. A 6-month term is 5% off the monthly price ($475 a month), and a 12-month term is 10% off ($450 a month).

How does 24/7 access work?

Members get in any time by keycard or phone app. There's no front desk. The building has cameras, and every member signs a waiver.

Can juniors train here?

Yes, through session packages. Minors train only with a parent or coach present. Contact us for details.

Why is membership limited?

Membership is limited to 133 members, so you can get on a station and your coaches know your plan.

What if I only want lessons or coaching?

Training-only packages are priced per hour and don't include facility access. See the training-only packages above.
